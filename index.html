What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet generally refers to a list used to organize product links and shopping notes around Oopbuy-related research. A useful entry identifies the seller, exact variant, measurements and checking date. This page explains that research process and directs category browsing to CAFANS.

Brand photographs are visual references, not CAFANS inventory listings or evidence of availability, authenticity or brand endorsement.

An Oopbuy shopping guide built around decisions

Before saving a link,
give it a reason to stay.

A long list of Oopbuy finds becomes more useful when every saved item answers a specific question. Organize the research around what you can establish.

01 /

What role does this item fill?

Choose a category and a purpose: an everyday shoe, a warmer layer, a training jersey or a finishing accessory. Record the color and shape you want before comparing alternatives.

02 /

Is this the exact version?

Oopbuy product links need context. Save the seller, model or style identifier, selected color and variant. Similar photographs can appear on listings with different materials, sizes or terms.

03 /

Can the fit be compared?

For an Oopbuy clothing spreadsheet, record garment measurements and compare them with clothing you already own. For an Oopbuy shoe spreadsheet, note the sizing system and any available model-specific measurements.

04 /

What still needs checking?

Separate the listing's claims from your observations. Mark unclear fabric composition, missing photographs, uncertain dimensions and unconfirmed costs as open questions before making a decision.

Read photographs as evidence of details.

Look for views that answer a question: outsole shape for shoes, stitching and seams for clothing, the adjustment mechanism on a cap, or the closure on a bracelet.

If inspection or QC photographs are available, confirm which item and variant they show. Images may help you assess visible details, but they cannot establish every material property or prove authenticity.

Keep reference photography separate from photographs of the actual item being evaluated.

Keep the budget open until costs are known.

A displayed item price is only one part of planning. Record applicable delivery charges, service fees, exchange-rate effects and destination taxes separately when they are known.

Planning total: item price + applicable delivery + service charges + currency costs + destination taxes.

Leave unknown amounts marked “pending.” Check the relevant provider's current quotation and terms before relying on an estimate.

Clear answers

Know what your research can tell you.

What should an Oopbuy spreadsheet contain?

Keep the product URL, seller, exact variant, size measurements, material description, listed price, estimated extra costs and date checked. Mark missing information instead of treating it as confirmed.

Does this page provide an official Oopbuy spreadsheet?

No. This is an independent research guide with links to CAFANS categories. It does not provide an official Oopbuy spreadsheet, live stock feed or ordering service.

How do I handle an old or broken product link?

Mark it as unavailable, record the date and search the relevant category again. Compare any replacement listing from the beginning because its seller, variant and terms may differ.

Can a brand photograph prove product authenticity?

No. A brand name, reference image or inspection photograph alone does not establish authenticity. Assess the seller and supporting documentation independently.

Where do the category buttons go?

The category buttons open their corresponding CAFANS category pages. General browsing buttons open the CAFANS homepage.
